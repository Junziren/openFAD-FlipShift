SPECTRAL TRANSFORMATION PROCESSOR

openFAD FlipShift

A creative spectral transformation effect in the openFAD family, developed by UnpureBloom for detailed frequency remapping and visual analysis.

openFADChinese-first open-source music visual toolset initiated by FAD Records.↗ UnpureBloomDeveloper and project channel.↗ Source repositoryCode, issue tracking and project history.↗

MAIN FUNCTIONS

  • 24 transformsShift, mirror, harmonic remapping, pitch mapping, glitch, phase and peak-relative modes.
  • Long waterfallFour visual LOOK profiles, adjustable speed and preserved spectral history.
  • Detailed analysisInput/output FFT, frequency guides, meters, freeze and quality control.
  • Host integrationVST3/WebView2 and AUv3/WKWebView build paths with automation gestures and state recall.

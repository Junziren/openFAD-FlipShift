# openFAD Logo Usage

## Core idea

The wordmark pairs an open, outlined prefix with a dense technical core. The amber datum marks a measured transition; in the compact mark it also stands in for the central axis between `F` and `D`.

## Files

- `openfad-primary.svg`: canonical full-color wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `openfad-primary-light.svg`: contrast-adjusted full-color wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `openfad-black.svg` and `openfad-white.svg`: one-color reproduction.
- `openfad-mark.svg`: compact mark for dark UI, avatars, favicons, and small controls.
- `openfad-mark-light.svg`: compact mark for light backgrounds.
- `openfad-mark-black.svg` and `openfad-mark-white.svg`: one-color compact marks.
- `openfad-flipshift-lockup.svg`: product relationship example; replace only the product descriptor in future lockups.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mint | `#54DDB7` | Canonical brand color on dark backgrounds. |
| Deep mint | `#16735E` | Contrast-safe mint substitute on light backgrounds. |
| Amber | `#EFAD3E` | Datum and pivot accent only. |
| Carbon | `#101416` | Primary dark background and black reproduction. |
| Mist | `#DBE3E3` | Open prefix and light foreground on dark backgrounds. |

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the wordmark's `o` stroke thickness on every side. Do not place the wordmark below 120 px wide in digital interfaces; use the compact mark instead. The compact mark is approved from 16 px square upward.

## Rules

- Use the supplied light-background variants rather than placing canonical mint directly on white.
- Keep the amber datum and the amber `A` crossbar together in full-color wordmarks.
- Use a one-color variant when color reproduction is uncertain.
- Preserve aspect ratio and transparent canvas margins.
- Do not typeset or redraw `openFAD` with a substitute font.
- Do not add gradients, glow, shadows, outlines, rotation, or additional spectrum graphics.
- Keep product names outside the compact mark.

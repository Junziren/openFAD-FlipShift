# Concept Scoring

Scores use a 0-10 scale and the weighted rubric from `$design-logo-system`.

| Concept | Recognition 30% | Small size 25% | Distinctiveness 20% | System fit 15% | Production 10% | Weighted result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pivot Stencil | 9.2 | 8.7 | 8.8 | 9.0 | 9.3 | **8.98** |
| Phase Rail | 7.2 | 5.8 | 8.3 | 7.0 | 6.6 | 6.98 |
| Open Spectrum | 8.6 | 8.9 | 7.4 | 8.8 | 9.0 | 8.51 |

## Selection

`Pivot Stencil` is selected. It leads by 0.47 points over `Open Spectrum`; the project tie-break rule applies at a difference below 0.50 and also favors Pivot Stencil. It keeps the industrial character inside the wordmark, avoids a generic standalone spectrum symbol, and produces the strongest product-lockup hierarchy.

`Phase Rail` remains an exploration only because its center split damages the glyphs at plugin-header scale. `Open Spectrum` remains an exploration only because the mirrored-bar symbol is less ownable despite its strong small-size behavior.

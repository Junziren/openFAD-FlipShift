openFAD / concept study

Industrial wordmark system
17 July 2026

01

Pivot Stencil

Contrasts an open monoline prefix with a compact stencil FAD. A single amber datum separates the public, open layer from the technical core.

02

Phase Rail

Splits every glyph into offset upper and lower phases. The amber rail is structural, producing a more kinetic and overtly signal-oriented identity.

03

Open Spectrum

Leads with mirrored spectrum bars and keeps the wordmark more conventional. This is the most symbol-led route and the easiest to separate from product names.

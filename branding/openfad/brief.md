# openFAD Logo Brief

## Brand role

openFAD is the master identity for an open audio-software initiative. Product names such as FlipShift sit beneath it as replaceable descriptors.

## Design intent

Build an industrial custom wordmark that feels precise, modular, and native to signal-processing tools. The identity should suggest measured transformation rather than generic music imagery.

## System requirements

- Preserve the exact capitalization `openFAD`.
- Prioritize digital interfaces while remaining suitable for websites, social profiles, covers, and print.
- Use mint `#54DDB7` as the primary brand signal and amber `#EFAD3E` only as a datum or pivot accent.
- Support full-color, black, and white reproduction.
- Keep all final glyphs vector-native with no font or raster dependency.
- Keep the compact mark legible at 16 px and the wordmark legible near 120 px wide.

## Avoid

- Generic waveform circles, headphones, musical notes, or equalizer clichés.
- Gradients, glow, shadows, and decorative effects that fail in one color.
- Fine gaps that disappear at plugin-header scale.
- Product-specific imagery inside the master-brand symbol.
